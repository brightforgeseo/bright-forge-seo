# Bright Forge AI search, GEO and AEO opportunity audit

Date: 2026-08-31
Owner URL: `/ai-search-optimization-services-philippines/`

## Decision

Keep one existing commercial owner and redesign it substantially. Do not create separate GEO, AEO, LLM SEO, ChatGPT SEO or AI Overview service routes. Those labels describe overlapping parts of the same buyer problem and separate pages would be thin, repetitive and likely to cannibalise the existing owner.

## Baseline

- Bright Forge owner URL tracked in AU, UK and US: 0 ranking keywords returned by SE Ranking.
- Qualified competitor landing-page evidence: 118 market-keyword rows.
- Unique qualified phrases: 92.
- Market rows: AU 33, UK 25, US 60.
- The former public page used a repetitive sequence of card grids and exposed a public monthly amount. Both conflicted with the approved design and private-pricing direction.

The 118 rows are competitor landing-page observations across markets, not combined demand and not 118 unique phrases.

## Primary-source guardrails

- Google says normal SEO best practices remain relevant for AI Overviews and AI Mode. It says no special schema or AI text file is required for inclusion.
- OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search separately from GPTBot training controls.
- Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance reports citations, cited URLs and sampled grounding queries. Citations do not indicate placement or importance.

## Design direction

Premium dark editorial AI visibility observatory. The memorable device is an illustrative citation path that shows buyer question, generated answer and source corroboration without fake client data. The page alternates dark technical sections with warm editorial surfaces and one deliberate orange measurement section. It uses ledger rows, editorial splits, an orbit diagram and platform table rather than repeated rounded card grids.

## Commercial boundaries

- British-led, Philippines-based, international delivery.
- No invented clients, citations, rankings, revenue or outcomes.
- No guarantee of inclusion in generated answers.
- No public amount, range, rate or minimum term.
- Pricing intent handled through scope factors and a private proposal.
- Citations, visits, tracked actions and confirmed leads remain separate metrics.
