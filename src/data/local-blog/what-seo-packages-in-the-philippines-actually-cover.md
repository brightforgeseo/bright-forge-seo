A package is a description of work. It is not a product on a shelf. Philippine buyers still meet SEO as Bronze, Silver and Gold, or as Starter, Growth and Enterprise, as if the difference were a list of extras. The useful difference is hours, skill, access and risk. Two proposals can use the same package name and still be different jobs.

Treat every package as a scope that must survive a simple test. Can you point to the pages, the diagnosis, the production load and the reporting that the fee buys this month? If the answer is a menu of labels, you are shopping for activity. If the answer names work that matches the site in front of you, you are buying a campaign.

## A Package Is A Scope Of Work

Scope answers four questions. What will be diagnosed. What will be changed on the site. What will be published or earned. How will anyone know it happened. A package that cannot answer those questions is a brochure.

Diagnosis is the part menus skip because it does not photograph well. A crawl, an indexation check, a look at Search Console, a review of the enquiry path, and a comparison against the pages that already attract commercial searches all belong before a content calendar. Without that, the package is a guess with a price.

Changes on the site are the part that actually reaches Google. Titles, headings, internal links, templates, local listings, schema where it is justified, and the copy on revenue pages. If the package never says who implements those changes, the hours will leak into tickets and waiting.

Publishing and earning are the visible workstreams. Service pages, location pages, supporting articles that earn their place, and mentions that a real reader might see. Volume is not the same as either of those.

Reporting closes the loop. Shipped URLs, Search Console movement on the queries that matter, and enquiry quality. A dashboard of vanity rankings is not a scope item. It is a sales prop.

## Why Menu Packages Mislead Buyers

Menus exist because they are easy to sell. They let a salesperson talk before anyone has opened the site. They also let two very different businesses receive the same PDF. A 15 page clinic site in Davao and a 3,000 URL catalogue in Metro Manila do not need the same extras. They need different diagnosis.

The usual extras are padding. Social posts, infographics, a fixed number of guest posts, unlimited keywords, and a weekly call that nobody uses. Those items make a table look full. They do not decide whether the contact form on the highest intent page works, or whether five location pages are cannibalising each other.

Unlimited is the most common trap word. Unlimited keywords usually means a rank tracker with a high limit. Unlimited content usually means thin posts produced to a quota. Unlimited support usually means a shared inbox. None of those is a substitute for a named list of URLs and a reason each one exists.

Guarantees sit next to menus because both sell certainty. Search placements are not inventory. A package that promises page one in 30 days is not a higher tier. It is a different product, and a worse one.

A honest package can still use a name. Call it a 90 day technical cleanup, or a local profile rebuild, or a commercial page rewrite. The name should describe the work, not a rung on a ladder.

## Market Bands For Philippine SEO Packages

Public market fees in 2026 still cluster, and those clusters are useful only as a map of hours. They are not a rate card for any one firm.

Very cheap monthly packages often sit around 5,000 to 20,000 PHP. That band is usually one person with a few hours. Metadata, a couple of posts, directory submissions, and a report copied from a tool. It can be an honest scope for a new local site if the owner writes the copy and can publish the same week. It cannot be a national campaign.

Entry agency packages commonly land between 20,000 and 50,000 PHP a month. Expect a junior team, a template audit, a fixed on-page list, and a PDF. Some of that work is real. Depth is the constraint. JavaScript rendering, thin category templates, and a messy Google Business Profile will be noted and then left, because the hours are already spoken for.

Mid market retainers usually fall between 50,000 and 120,000 PHP. That is the first band where audit, content, selective authority work and reporting against Search Console can share a month without one of them becoming theatre. It is also the band where a scope should name priorities rather than recycle a 20 point checklist.

From 150,000 PHP upwards the market is pricing complexity. Larger sites, several markets or languages, heavier production, developer coordination, and reporting a board can read. That is not a prestige surcharge by default. It is specialist time and the cost of being wrong on a property that already has traffic to lose.

If a quote sits far below the band that matches the site, the missing hours come from somewhere. They come from skipped diagnosis, spun copy, bulk links, or reports about keywords nobody searches. ## What A Usable Scope Names

Ask for URLs. A usable scope names the homepage, the money service pages, the location or category templates, and any blog or resource URLs that already attract clicks. If the vendor cannot name them before month two, they have not looked.

Ask for owners. Who crawls. Who writes. Who talks to the developer. Who can change the CMS. Who signs off legally sensitive copy. A package that says "our team" without roles is hiding a junior generalist.

Ask for volume that can be inspected. Number of pages rewritten, number of new pages, number of templates touched, expected outreach attempts, and the reporting artefacts you will receive. Volume without quality rules is still a quota. Volume with a review step is a production plan.

Ask for access. Search Console, analytics, the CMS, Google Business Profile, and staging if it exists. A package that starts without those is working from the public HTML only. That can be enough for a first look. It is not enough for a year of claims.

Ask for what will not be done. A good scope refuses bulk directory blasts, guaranteed rankings, and content the business cannot stand behind. Refusal is a quality signal. A menu that says yes to every extra is selling compliance, not judgement.

## Starter Scopes And Their Honest Limits

A starter scope can be useful when the site is small, the offer is clear, and the owner can implement. Typical honest contents are a short technical pass, title and heading repairs on a handful of URLs, a corrected Google Business Profile, and a monthly note that lists what changed.

The limit is parallel work. One person cannot rebuild information architecture, write commercial pages, recover a spammy link profile, and argue with a theme developer in the same month at that fee. If the salesperson says they can, they are describing a different month from the one you will receive.

Starter scopes fail when they are asked to do mid market work. National ecommerce, multi location home services, and sites already damaged by a previous vendor all need more than a metadata sweep. Buying the starter package as a trial on those properties is not thrift. It is a delay with a monthly debit.

An honest trial is a defined sprint with a written output, not a discounted Gold package. Crawl, Search Console, conversion path on the top URLs, and a 90 day plan. If that document is specific, a longer scope can follow. If it could belong to any business, stop.

## Mid Market Scopes That Can Run In Parallel

Once a site needs more than one workstream, the package has to describe sequence. Technical blockers first if the site cannot be crawled or indexed cleanly. Then the revenue pages. Then supporting content that fills a real gap. Authority work only where the page already deserves a mention.

Parallel does not mean everything at once. It means the team can keep a crawl issue, a rewrite, and a reporting cycle in motion without dropping one of them every time a blog quota is due. That is the practical meaning of a mixed team.

Local work belongs in the scope if the business is found on Maps. Profile completeness, categories, services, photos that match the premises, review replies, and NAP consistency with the website. A package that treats local as a directory add-on will leave the listing and the site arguing with each other.

Ecommerce scopes need template language. Category and product templates, faceted navigation, duplicate titles, and internal links from commercial categories to the products that should rank. A blog calendar is not an ecommerce package.

International or multi market scopes need language, hreflang or a documented decision against it, and a clear statement of which country the work is for. A Philippine team can run that work. The scope still has to name the market, not assume Manila English will travel.

## What Unlimited And Guaranteed Packages Hide

Unlimited keywords hide a tool. You can add 500 terms to a tracker in an afternoon. Tracking is not progress. The scope should say which queries are in play because they match an offer and a page, and which are parked.

Unlimited backlinks hide a network. Real mentions are negotiated one property at a time. A package that prices links by the hundred is buying inventory. The inventory is usually directories, private blogs, or sitewide footers. Those can be ignored later, or they can become a cleanup job.

Unlimited content hides a generator. Forty similar posts a month will compete with each other and with the service pages you actually need. A usable content scope names the page type, the proof required, and who checks facts. Word count is not a quality rule.

Guaranteed rankings hide a method the vendor does not want to describe, or a contract written so that any client delay voids the promise. Either way you are paying for certainty that search does not sell. Forecast ranges based on current impressions and page quality are a different conversation. They belong in a proposal as judgement, not as a clause.

## How To Compare Two Packages For The Same Site

Do not compare the number of deliverables. Compare the commercial job. Which services should produce enquiries. Which cities or countries. Which pages already attract clicks. What a lead is worth. Who will implement. How long the business can wait for directional movement.

Then line up the two scopes against that job. Which one names the blockers. Which one puts hours into the revenue URLs rather than into a blog nobody asked for. Which one reports from Search Console and the enquiry trail. Which one refuses work that would create risk.

Price is a later column. A cheaper package that funds reversible work can be a rational first step. A cheaper package that funds spam links or mass generated location pages is not cheap, because the next team will spend months unwinding it.

[SEO services in the Philippines](/seo-services-philippines/) should read like joined up work around that commercial job. If the package still looks like a buffet after you have sent the brief, the vendor is selling a menu.

Give the chosen scope time, but not a blank year. Technical repairs can show in crawl stats within weeks. Content and authority take longer. Three months is a fair window to judge whether the work is real. It is not always a fair window to judge whether the business has won search. Anyone who collapses those two timelines into a package benefit is selling speed they cannot deliver.

## When A Package Should Be Refused

Refuse a package that will not grant Search Console access. You cannot verify the story.

Refuse a package that keeps the domain, the CMS, or the ads account in the vendor's name. That is lock in, not delivery.

Refuse a package that will not name URLs by the end of discovery. Strategy without pages is a slide deck.

Refuse a package that prices links by the hundred or content by the tonne without a review step you can see.

Refuse a package that treats every business as the same Gold tier with more posts. Your site is not a menu row.

A refused package is not a lost month. It is the month you did not spend on work that would have to be undone. If you already know the site is messy, pay for diagnosis before a long retainer. If the site is healthy and the gap is thin service pages, put the fee into writing and internal links. The package should follow that mix, not the other way around.
