Australian local SEO can be run from Manila. Google Business Profile cannot be invented from Manila. That distinction is the whole job. A Philippines team can audit listings, clean NAP, write suburb pages that match how people actually search, build review processes, and keep google.com.au landing pages honest. We cannot, and will not, create a fake Australian office so a profile looks local.

We are a Quezon City team. Timezones with Sydney and Melbourne are usable. Language for Australian English is something we already produce. None of that substitutes for Google's rule that a Business Profile has to represent a business that customers can visit, or a business that travels to customers from a real operating location. If you do not have that presence in Australia, you do not have a GBP problem to "optimise". You have an eligibility problem.

## What Google Still Requires On The Ground

Google's guidelines for representing a business say you can create a profile if you have a physical location customers can visit, or you travel to customers where they are. The profile has to reflect the business as it is recognised in the real world: signage, stationery, name, address, and categories that describe the core work. One business gets one profile. Keyword-stuffed names, extra locations that do not exist, and virtual addresses are how listings get suspended.

For storefronts, that means a precise street address, hours staff actually keep, and a place a customer could turn up. For service-area businesses, that means an operating address that is real, often hidden if you do not want the public at the door, plus service areas that match where you actually travel. PO boxes are not a storefront. A virtual office with no staff during hours is not a storefront. A coworking hot desk with no permanent signage and no staff of yours on site is not a storefront. We will not file those as "local SEO tactics".

Service areas have limits. Google's published guidance has been that you should not treat a whole country or state as a casual service area, and that the overall area should stay within a realistic driving time of where the business is based. A plumber in Geelong does not get a national profile because a Philippines agency is willing to write 40 suburb pages. The pages would be the smaller lie. The profile would be the one Google can suspend.

Australian records sit in the background of this. If ABR details, the website footer, invoices, and the profile disagree on name or address, you have given reviewers and algorithms a mismatch. We will ask for the trading name, the address you can verify, and the phone that rings to your business, not a call centre that cannot confirm jobs.

## How The Distance Is Managed In Practice

Manila is two hours behind Sydney and Melbourne for much of the year, three during daylight saving, two behind Brisbane, and on Perth time. That is enough overlap to run a weekly call in AEST and to ship profile edits the same Australian working day. It is not enough to stand on the footpath and photograph your new sign. You still take the photos. You still confirm the hours on a public holiday. You still tell us when a van is off the road in a service suburb.

Verification is yours. Postcard, video, phone, or whatever Google offers for that listing is a proof of the location, not a proof of the SEO supplier. We can prepare the profile fields and the supporting documents list. We cannot pretend to occupy the address. If Google asks for a video walk-through, that is staff on site, not a Zoom from Quezon City with a stock image.

Multi-location brands need a spreadsheet of truth before anyone logs into 12 profiles. Each location: legal name as used on the door, address format identical to signage, phone that belongs to that site, primary category, service area or storefront flag, landing page URL, and who is allowed to reply to reviews. A Philippines team can operate that sheet. A marketing intern creating "Sydney SEO office" listings for an agency that is not in Sydney cannot.

## Suburb Pages And Maps Packs Are Not The Same Lever

Australian businesses often want a page per suburb because a competitor has them. Sometimes those pages rank for "plumber near me" style queries. Sometimes they are thin clones that Google folds together. We will look at search demand, your real coverage, and whether a service landing URL plus a clean profile already covers the query.

If we do build location pages, each one needs a reason to exist: a real service pattern, a landing from ads or GBP, a distinct proof, and internal links that do not look like a generated grid. Distance language has to be true. "We are based in X and we regularly take jobs in Y" is allowed when the diary shows it. "Our Melbourne office" is not allowed when there is no Melbourne office. We will not write the second sentence for you, including when the writer is in the Philippines and the temptation is to sound closer.

Maps pack visibility still leans on relevance, distance, and prominence. A remote SEO team can improve relevance and prominence: categories, reviews, links, on-page, citations. We cannot move your pin. If you are a service-area business hiding the address, proximity still relates to where you operate from. Buying a virtual mailbox in a CBD to game the pin is a suspension risk, not a growth hack.

## Red Flags When The Supplier Is Offshore

If a vendor offers to "set up your Google profile in Australia" without asking where you trade, they are selling a listing, not a business. If they propose an address they control, stop. If they want to create extra profiles for suburbs you do not staff, stop. If they write location pages that claim offices in cities where you have a freight depot and a locked gate, stop.

If they never mention suspension risk, they have not done this work. If they treat citations as a 500-pack of spam directories, they will make NAP worse. If they cannot show how Australian English will be edited, you will get US spelling on a .com.au page and look like a template farm.

We will also push back on review gating that hides bad feedback, and on keyword names that Google has been policing for years. "Smith Plumbing Melbourne 24/7 Best" is not a business name. It is a policy problem waiting for a reviewer.
